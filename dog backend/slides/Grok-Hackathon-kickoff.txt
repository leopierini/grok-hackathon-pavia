      HOW WE W ORK TOGETHER



Code of Conduct

                                                                       Respect everyone
                                                                       Be kind and inclusive. No harassment or discrimination, in person or online.


                                                                       Take care of the museum
                                                                       The exhibits are historic. Handle with care and follow staff instructions.


                                                                       Safety first with hardware
                                                                       Robots, arms and drones only in your slot and always with staff.


                                                                       Play fair
                                                                       Only public, synthetic or company data. Credit your sources.
    Build boldly.
    Treat everyone well.                                               Speak up
                                                                       If something feels wrong, tell the organisers or museum staff right away.
