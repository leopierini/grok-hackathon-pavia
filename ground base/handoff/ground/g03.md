## Behavior

`SessionStore` admits one incident into IDLE. A second open, a second start, and a second `report_saved` are rejected. Mission phase, physical status, report outcome, and the recovery/flight/halt locks stay separate fields. Arming is one-use and is invalidated on halt. Arrival and flight-ready are stage-scoped: an early event id stays rejected when the stage later becomes current. Route completion sets route flags and leaves `arrival_confirmed` null. Unknown tri-state stays `None` and faults; the strings `"true"` and `"false"` are rejected. Ethernet versus Wi-Fi is stored on the snapshot and is not switched on link loss. Wi-Fi does not arm. A real dog rejects simulated arrival; a real drone rejects simulated flight-ready. Halt and link loss latch. Recovery commits only when `verified is True` and the kernel generation is a fresh uncancelled epoch. Snapshots are frozen dataclass views; decision and evidence maps are mapping proxies. Replay of the same applied events into a second store compares equal.

## Extra edge cases

- Two `permission_consumed` events applied from two threads admit one mission.
- Duplicate event ids, including a rejected early confirmation, do not apply a second time.
- Sequence values must be positive integers. Booleans are rejected because `bool` is an `int`.
- A future-epoch ticket while the authority is not cancelled is `stale_ticket` from `require_current`. A ticket issued before `latch_cancel` is `cancelled`.
- A real drone with a fake dog rejects `launch_origin=dog_pad`.
- A payload that names another zone or target does not replace `incident.zone` or `incident.target`.
- Link loss sets `transport_fallback` false and leaves `dog_transport` unchanged.
- Unknown flight state after a captured frame keeps the frame path, sets FAULT, and a later resolution cannot clear it.
- Opening the next mission while a flight lock or recovery lock is held is rejected until verified recovery.

## Limitations
